Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

1. Data Collection

EAMA Garments collects specific business-related data necessary for the execution of industrial manufacturing contracts. This includes corporate identity information, technical specifications, and procurement schedules required for global logistics coordination.

All digital assets, including technical drawings and pattern files, are stored within our high-security encrypted cloud infrastructure to ensure integrity throughout the production lifecycle.

2. Client Confidentiality

We maintain strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) with every partner. Intellectual property, proprietary garment construction methods, and exclusive fabric blends are treated as highly classified manufacturing secrets.

Access to specific project data is restricted to authorized personnel on a "need-to-know" basis, ensuring your brand's competitive advantage is protected from the concept phase through to final delivery.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Data sharing is strictly limited to verified logistics partners and material suppliers essential to the fulfillment of your order. No client data is ever sold or utilized for third-party marketing purposes.

Transparency reports regarding our supply chain and ethical compliance are available upon request through the Partner Portal, ensuring complete visibility without compromising individual project security.
